How the estimate works. Annual cost = supply charge (c/day × 365) + grid-import energy − solar feed-in credit, computed per plan from the AER's published rates. Solar generation = system size × Adelaide yield. Battery raises how much of your own solar you use instead of exporting, bounded by usable capacity, daytime export surplus and evening load. Time-of-use plans split your import across peak/shoulder/off-peak using a typical household profile.

Assumptions you can tune above change every number live. These are estimates for comparison, GST-inclusive — not a quote or financial advice. Confirm any plan on its official document (linked in each plan's detail) before switching, since discounts, demand charges, controlled-load and eligibility conditions can shift the real figure. Data source: AER Consumer Data Right Energy PRD — the same plans shown on energymadeeasy.gov.au.
